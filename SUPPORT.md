# Support / 支持

Website: https://yinghe.org

Public software issues: https://github.com/jo3sheng/yinghe-studio/issues

Orders, activation, refunds, device replacement and sensitive reports: licenses@yinghe.org

Before reporting a bug, note the app version, operating system, chosen encoder, reproduction steps and exact error. Remove names, local paths, registration codes, email addresses and tokens from screenshots and logs. Share private media only through an agreed private support channel.

反馈软件问题请附版本、系统、编码器、复现步骤及错误信息；公开日志前清理个人路径、邮箱、注册码及密钥。订单、退款、激活和换机问题请通过邮箱处理。
